### The McNamara Fallacy

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*yUTAgnaRzFu0_l1B.png)

> First step is to measure what can be easily measured. This is okay as far as it goes.

> The second is to disregard that which can’t easily be measured or to give it an arbitrary quantitative value.

> The third step is to presume that what can’t be measured easily isn’t important. ***This is Blindness***.

> The forth step is to say what can’t easily be measured really doesn’t exist. ***This is suicide***.

*Charles Handy, The Empty Rain Coat*
