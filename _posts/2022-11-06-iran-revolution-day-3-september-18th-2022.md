### Iran Revolution. Day 3 , September 18th 2022

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*tuym3QMX9cxq_yrd.jpg)*Woman protesting for the death of Mahsa Amini, not wearing the mandatory hijab!*

September 18th, 2022 is when I can find the first time the chant “Woman, Life, Freedom” is used in protests!

### “Woman, Life, Freedom” revolution started!

Mahsa Amini has become a symbol for women’s freedom. The slogan that is now spreading throughout Iran and the world is: Woman, Life, Freedom.

At this point of the revolution, there are protests in all major cities and universities. Some celebrities and famous athletes also started to join the revolution!

> During protests against the murder of [#Mahsa\_Amini](https://twitter.com/hashtag/Mahsa_Amini?src=hashtag_click), the police and security forces in Sanandaj, Kurdistan province, fired on the protesters.

> In Tehran, protesters marched against the murder of [#Mahsa\_Amini](https://twitter.com/hashtag/Mahsa_Amini?src=hashtag_click). “Woman, Life, Freedom,” they chanted.
