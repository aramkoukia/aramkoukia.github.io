### #Vancouver, Grouse Mountain on a Friday Night

It has been snowing in Vancouver (Yeah for real) this past week and it’s been hard to get around with car in the city, simply because people and the city get so excited about the snow, since we don’t get much of it over here.

![](/assets/img/2016-12-10-vancouver-grouse-mountain-on-a-friday-night/1.gif)*Meanwhile in #Vancouver*

On Friday night, we were invited to [ResponsTek’s](http://www.responsetek.com/) annual Christmas party on top of the [Grouse Mountain](https://www.grousemountain.com/), which turned out to be great.

[ResponseTek](http://www.responsetek.com/) had arranged a shuttle for people who want to drink or don’t want to drive in bad weather in Robson Square and before I get to the shuttle, had a bit of time to kill so I went for a walk around there, and as always, Vancouver was LOVELY.

And people were like:

![](/assets/img/2016-12-10-vancouver-grouse-mountain-on-a-friday-night/2.gif)

I was a little worried that I might get stuck in the mountain because of the heavy snow.

As a matter of fact, some of my friends had to spend the night in the restaurant up there last year because the gondola was shut down because of the bad weather condition.

![](/assets/img/2016-12-10-vancouver-grouse-mountain-on-a-friday-night/3.gif)

But I gotta admit, the beauty of this place, made me completely forget about the bad weather.

The only regret I have is, we forgot to take proper snow gears with us to go for a snowball fight up there…

![](/assets/img/2016-12-10-vancouver-grouse-mountain-on-a-friday-night/4.gif)*Our next year’s after party…*
