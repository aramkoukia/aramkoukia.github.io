### Upstream by: Dan Heath

We often find ourselves reacting to problems, putting out fires, dealing with emergencies. We should shift our attention to preventing them.

### 7 Questions to ask yourself:

1. **How to get early warning of the problem?**

> Deploy sensors

> Look for predictors

> Watch for false positives

1. **How to know you are succeeding?**

> Pre-game your measures

> Use paired measures

> Watch for Ghost Victories

1. **How to avoid doing harm?**

> Look beyond the immediate

> Close feedback loop

> Watch for failure to test and overconfidence

1. **Who will pay for what doesn’t happen?**

> Align incentives

> Stitch together pockets of value

> Watch for the wrong pocket problem

1. **How to unite the right people?**

> Surround the problem

> Use data for learning

> Watch for data for inspection

1. **How to change the system?**

> Fight for systems change

> Shape the water

> Watch for enabling a flawed system

1. **Where to find a point of leverage?**

> Get closer to the problem

> Watch for notion that prevention must save money

### Barriers to overcome:

1. **Tunneling:** “I can’t deal with that right now”
2. **Lack of ownership:** *“That’s not my problem to solve“*
3. **Problem Blindness:** *“I don’t see the problem, or, it seems inevitable”*
