### Top Paying Technologies and Programming Languages in 2017

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1200/1*eyj62id3no4e7p2SETyZ3A.jpeg)

In case you missed it, [Stack Overflow](http://stackoverflow.com) released the 2017 Developer survey results and as always there are really interesting information there.

I wanted to take a moment and point out some parts of it that is about the “**Top Paying Technologies**” and in case you are looking to study something in your free time, you can pick from that list as some of them are stuff that are relatively newer technologies and trending.

The Top Paying Technologies are categorized into 5, and seems like, in US, UK, Germany, France and Worldwide, different things are on the top of the list.

**United States:** [Go](https://golang.org/), [Scala](https://www.scala-lang.org/) and Objective-C are the top 3.

**Worldwide:** [Clojure](http://clojure.com/), [Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org/en-US/) and [Elixir](http://elixir-lang.org/) are the top 3.

**United Kingdom:** [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/), Ruby and C# are the top 3.

**Germany:** Java, [Python](https://www.python.org/), TypeScript are the top 3.

**France:** Python, C++ and JavaScript are the top 3.
