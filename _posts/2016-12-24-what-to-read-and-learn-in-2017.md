### What to read and learn in 2017

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*h6QsbzYwny5FwyuRZ6bMaA.gif)

Yes it is that time of year again. The new years resolution and that bullshit.

To be clear from the beginning, don’t try to learn or read all of this, because then you will be like: *“What the fuck was I thinking?”* before the end of the first month.

This is just a list to pick few items from:

### Front-end technologies:

- [ES2017](https://github.com/tc39/proposals/blob/master/finished-proposals.md)
- [TypeScript 2.1](https://blogs.msdn.microsoft.com/typescript/2016/12/07/announcing-typescript-2-1/)
- [AngularJs 2](https://angular.io/)
- [Vue.js](https://medium.com/the-vue-point/vue-2-0-is-here-ef1f26acf4b8)
- [Aurelia](http://aurelia.io/)
- [EmberJs](http://emberjs.com/)
- [SASS](http://sass-lang.com/) and [LESS](http://lesscss.org/)

### Back-end technologies:

- [C# 7.0](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/articles/core/index)
- [.Net Core](https://www.microsoft.com/net/core#windowsvs2015)
