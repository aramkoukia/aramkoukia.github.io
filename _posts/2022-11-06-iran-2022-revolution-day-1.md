### Iran Revolution. Day 1 , September 16th, 2022.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*MD3RZ8VSW4Bgf9yb.png)*Picture of Mahsa (Zhina) Amini. She was brutally beaten to death by so called Morality Police in Tehran, Iran. September 14, 2022.*

#### Tuesday September 22nd, 20:22 Tehran local timezone Iran’s 2022 revolution started!

Mahsa Amini, this 22 year young girl was from Saqqez a small city in Kurdistan province. She was visiting Tehran with her brother on September 16th 2022, where she was arrested by so called “Morality Police” for not wearing her headscarf “properly” and some of her hair was visible!

The so called “Morality Police” took her to custody, beat her to death and put her in coma and braindead (Left picture above is Mahsa in the hospital after she went to coma).

When this news went viral in social media, the Iran’s 2022 revolution started!

I am going to document some of the major events that happened each day since the start of the revolution in a series of posts.

> Zhina Amini, 22, of Saqqez, who traveled to Tehran with her family, was arrested by the admonishing patrol police. Zhina has a stroke at the police station a few hours later and is rushed to the hospital by ambulance. Her family reports that doctors are despondent about her.

> Photo of [#Mahsa\_Amini](https://twitter.com/hashtag/Mahsa_Amini?src=hashtag_click) (Zhina) in the hospital, 22 years old. As a result of physical violence against her, she was taken to the hospital after being arrested yesterday by the admonishing patrol police. She is in a coma, and the doctors seem despondent about her health.

Islamic Republic government predicted protests, so on September 16th, they started to shut down the internet in the country!

> Confirmed: A significant internet outage has been registered in Tehran, [#Iran](https://twitter.com/hashtag/Iran?src=hashtag_click) with real time network data showing connectivity at 67% of ordinary levels; the incident comes amid protests over the death of Mahsa Amini and may affect coverage of events on the ground

Kasra Hospital Announcement: At 20:22 on Tuesday, September 16th, [#Mahsa\_Amini](https://twitter.com/hashtag/Mahsa_Amini?src=hashtag_click) was referred to the hospital “without vital signs and in a state of brain death” and, despite cardiac resuscitation, he suffered a cardiac arrest again on Friday and died.
