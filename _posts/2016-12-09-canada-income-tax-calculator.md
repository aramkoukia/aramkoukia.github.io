### Canada Income tax calculator

Recently I played a bit with Apache Cordova and how you can build cross platform Apps, and this is the sample project I did using pure JavaScript and put it on Windows Store targeted for Windows 10 as well as Azure Websites.

The App is pretty simple. You can simply select the Year and the Province you live in and enter your annual income and the App will calculate the taxes for you and your net income after taxes.

Here is the link to the Azure Website:  
[Http://canadatax.azurewebsites.net](http://canadatax.azurewebsites.net/)

And this is where you can get the App from the Windows Store:  
<https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/store/apps/canada-tax/9nblggh6hblw>
