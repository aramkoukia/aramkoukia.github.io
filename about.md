I'm a **software alchemist**. Where others see chaos, I see the raw materials for breakthrough
solutions. But more than that, I'm someone who believes in bringing a little joy to the world,
one smile at a time. In big tech and beyond, I've led high-performing teams not just through
technical expertise, but by creating environments where laughter lives alongside excellence
and people genuinely love what they do.

I see everyone as a friend and believe the best code comes from teams that trust each other
enough to laugh together. My approach blends technical leadership with a people-first approach,
inspiring engineers, and helping teams focus on what truly matters while never losing sight of
the human connection that makes great work possible.

> Life becomes lighter when we choose humor and kindness in everyday moments.

I've found that teams who can laugh together build products that truly change the world.
Colleagues seek me out not just for technical guidance, but because sometimes you need someone
who helps you forget your worries and remember why you fell in love with building things in the
first place.

A champion of **agility and outcome-driven work**, I help teams cut waste, scale excellence, and
find joy in the journey, because the best results come when people are genuinely excited about
what they're creating.

Outside of work, you'll find me golfing ⛳️, playing soccer ⚽️ or tennis 🎾, hiking 🥾, strumming
guitar 🎸, writing, reading 📖, or watching comedy 📺, always looking for the next reason to smile.
